How to read it

  • Knee asymmetry at bottom of pump: front knee angle minus back knee angle in the frames where the back knee is most bent. A big positive number is the forum picture. The back leg squats and the front leg stays straight.
  • Front knee deepest bend: the 10th percentile front knee angle. If it stays above about 150 degrees the front leg never really loads.
  • Leg drive ratio: back knee range of motion divided by front knee range of motion. Near 1.0 means both legs pump. Well above 1.0 means the back leg pumps and the front leg is a strut.
  • Stance width: ankle to ankle as a fraction of leg length. Forum guidance is shoulder width or narrower.
  • Hip position: 0 is over the back ankle, 1 is over the front ankle. It is the best available proxy for the weight split between the feet.
  • The mast is rarely in shot, so foot position relative to the mast is not measured. In every side-on reference clip the back foot sits roughly over the mast and the front foot 20 to 30 percent of board length ahead, in both groups. The difference between pros and beginners shows in the knees and hips.
